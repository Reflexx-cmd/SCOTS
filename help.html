Live QueueKitchen wait time —
Log in, browse the menu, add items to your cart and choose Immediate (soonest pickup, shows a Checkout ETA you confirm) or Scheduled (a later 15-minute window, at least 30 minutes away). Pay by UPI, or Cash-at-Pickup for Immediate Orders. You get a token and a locked Token ETA. You can cancel before the order is Ready.
UPI works for every order. Cash-at-Pickup is for Immediate Orders only; Scheduled Orders are prepaid by UPI. Accounts with 2 No-Shows in 30 days are limited to UPI. An unpaid Pending Due blocks new orders until cleared.
Windows are 15 minutes long, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Each window has limited kitchen capacity (default 20 prep-minutes) and must start at least 30 minutes from now. A Scheduled Order stays hidden from the kitchen until its release time. If it starts late you get a revised ETA, and if the delay passes the grace period you can cancel for a 100% refund.
The live queue wait time is the estimated amount of kitchen prep time currently ahead in the active queue. SCOTS adds the prep time of every order that is still being received or prepared. It increases when a new order enters the kitchen queue and decreases when an order is completed or otherwise leaves the active queue. The same live value is shared across customers, canteen staff, admins and the token display.
A Ready order waits 20 minutes. If not collected it expires and a No-Show is logged. Prepaid orders forfeit the configured share (default 100%). An unpaid Cash-at-Pickup order adds that amount to your Pending Due.
Two No-Shows within 30 days restrict you to UPI prepayment. The restriction lifts automatically once the count drops below two, or after Admin review.
Your account is temporarily suspended and you cannot place orders. To restore access, contact the canteen office or use the appeal form below — an admin can reinstate your account after a quick chat.
Default policy keeps 100% of a prepaid order that expires uncollected; the Admin can configure a lower share and the remainder is refunded. Orders cancelled by the canteen (sold-out item, closing time) or delayed past the grace period are refunded in full.
Sold-out items are restocked by the canteen staff, usually within the hour during service. The menu updates instantly for everyone the moment an item is restocked.
