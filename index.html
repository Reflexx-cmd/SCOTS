The Flow

How SCOTS works

Four steps between you and a hot meal — designed for a 20-minute lecture break.

1

Order ahead

Browse the live menu, add items to your cart or hit Buy Now, and pay by UPI (Cash-at-Pickup for Immediate Orders).

2

Queue or schedule

Place an Immediate Order for the earliest ETA, or reserve a 15-minute pickup window for later today.

3

Get your token

A token number with a locked ETA is generated instantly — it never shifts once your order is placed.

4

Collect & go

Watch the canteen token display, walk up when your number is called, and collect. That's it.

Why SCOTS

Built for the campus rush hour

The 12:30 lunch rush used to mean a 25-minute queue. SCOTS spreads demand across the day with scheduled slots and a live kitchen queue.

  • Locked ETAs — the time you're promised never changes after ordering
  • Slot capacity keeps the kitchen realistic, not overloaded
  • Fair-play no-show policy keeps tokens honest for everyone
  • Sold-out items update everywhere in real time
Pickup Slots

15-minute windows, 09:00 – 18:00

Every slot carries a prep-minute capacity (by default 20 minutes of kitchen time). As orders reserve capacity, the slot meter fills — when it's full, SCOTS automatically suggests the nearest open slot.

Token System

One token, zero confusion

Your token (like SCOTS-104) follows the order from Received → Preparing → Ready → Collected. The public display board calls out ready tokens, and your Orders page shows the live status with a collect-by countdown.
